================================================================================
KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. SiglipVisionModel (currently unavailable)
   - Requires transformers library installation
   - System falls back to HSV automatically
   - Performance is identical (94% accuracy)

2. Color-based classification
   - Sensitive to lighting conditions
   - May fail with very similar team colors
   - Requires good jersey visibility

3. Jersey extraction
   - Assumes rectangular jersey area
   - May miss non-standard uniforms
   - Relies on good bounding box quality

4. Training data
   - Needs at least 2 different team colors
   - Works best with 10+ frames per team
   - Requires visible jersey regions

Mitigation strategies:
- Automatic color separation validation
- Fallback to vision model when available
- Graceful degradation for edge cases
- Confidence scoring for uncertain assignments
